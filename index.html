MARGARET TSAI
CV CONTACT

b. 2004

Born in California, studied abroad in Taiwan and Denmark, and currently based in New York, Margaret Tsai is a designer with a global perspective on the intersection public architecture and environmental design that prioritizes the triple bottom line: social, economic, and environmental sustainability.

As a senior at Cornell University, Margaret is majoring in Design + Environmental Analysis with minors in Architecture and Apparel Design.

Professional Experience:

  • Exhibition Design and Outreach at Albert R. Mann Library
  • Exhibit Development at Chenango County Museum
  • Exhibit Design at The Metropolitan Museum of Art
  • Exhibit Design at the Johnson Museum of Art
  • Researcher at the Hybrid Body Lab at Cornell University
  • Architectural Design at King’s Town Construction
  • Interior Design at Chain10 Architecture & Design
